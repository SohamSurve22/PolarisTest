## Metric Definitions

- **Positive class**: `covered` (obligation is fully addressed in the policy).
- **Negative class**: anything that is not `covered` (`partial`, `missing`, `undetermined`, `violation`, `conflict`).
- **Precision**: TP / (TP + FP)  — of all obligations predicted covered, fraction actually covered.
- **Recall**: TP / (TP + FN)  — of all obligations that are truly covered, fraction predicted covered.
- **False-covered count** (FP): obligations predicted covered but actually not.
- **False-covered rate**: FP / (FP + TN)  — among truly-not-covered obligations, fraction wrongly predicted covered.  Denominator is all true negatives + false positives.  This is the specificity complement (1 − specificity).
- **False-negative rate**: FN / (FN + TP)  — among truly covered obligations, fraction wrongly predicted non-covered.
- **Element F1**: micro-F1 over requirement-element satisfaction flags.
