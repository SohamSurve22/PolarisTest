# Dataset Card

## Dataset Name
PolarisLex Privacy Compliance Evaluation Dataset (Research)

## Version
0.1.0 (pilot)

## Purpose
Evaluate requirement-element decomposition and denial-polarity detection for reducing false-covered errors in automated privacy compliance auditing.

## Scope
Privacy policy clauses evaluated against Indian privacy law obligations (DPDP Act, SPDI Rules, CERT-In, IT Act) as modeled in the PolarisLex system.

## Source
To be documented as cases are added. Prefer official policy pages.

## Licensing
TBD - respect source terms

## Limitations
- Small pilot dataset
- Researcher-annotated labels, not reviewed by legal experts
- May not be representative of all policy styles
- Focuses on specific obligations modeled in the system

## Intended Use
Research evaluation only, not for production deployment decisions

## Ethical Considerations
No private user data. Only public policy documents or synthetic examples.
