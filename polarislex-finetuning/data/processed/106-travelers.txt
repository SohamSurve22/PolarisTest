## Online privacy statement

Revised April 9, 2025

The Travelers Indemnity Company and its affiliates ("Travelers") value your privacy. This Online Privacy Statement will inform you about how we use and disclose information that we and our vendors collect from your visits to travelers.com and other websites (the “Websites”) and software applications (the “Apps”) that we own or operate and that link to this Online Privacy Statement. Collectively, the Websites and the Apps are the “Services”.

This Online Privacy Statement only applies to information collected through the Services. If you have purchased or applied for an insurance product from us, please also see our Privacy Statement for Individual U.S. Personal Insurance Consumers, which applies to the information that we collect about policyholders and applicants, through the Services as well as any other medium. Travelers does business in a number of different jurisdictions. Additional Privacy Statements may apply depending on your jurisdiction or customer relationship.

### Personal information

“Personal Information” is information that identifies or is reasonably capable of being associated with you. Personal Information may in certain circumstances include such information as name, address, phone number, email address, credit history, social security number, driver’s license number, credit or debit card number, policy or claim information, automobile and home information, health information, username and passwords for online accounts, telematics, and geolocation data. Personal Information may also include information such as IP address, browsing history and other internet activity information collected when you use our Services. If you apply for a job or register with our career center, Personal Information may, in certain circumstances, also include information about your work and educational history, and other information you choose to provide in connection with your application.

Additionally, depending on the App and your device and application permission settings, the Apps may have access to certain information or features on your device, such as calendar events, geolocation (approximate and precise), media and files (including photos), camera, microphone, and phone and call information. If you do not permit the Apps to have such access, the Apps, including portions or features, may not function properly or as expected.

If you submit any Personal Information relating to other people to us or to our vendors, you represent that you have the authority to do so and to permit us to use the information in accordance with this Online Privacy Statement.

### Tracking technologies

We, as well as vendors operating on our behalf and other third parties, may collect information about you and your use of the Services using cookies, Flash objects, web beacons, pixel tags, embedded scripts, and other tracking technologies, such as:

- IP address

- Browser and device information, such as your device identifier, screen resolution, operating system name and version, device manufacturer and model, language, Internet browser type and version and the name and version of the Services (such as an App) you are using.

- App usage data, such as the date and time the App on your device accesses our servers and the information and files that have been downloaded to the App based on your device number

- General geolocation. We may collect the general geolocation of your device by, for example, using your IP address and satellite, cell phone tower or WiFi signals. We may also share your device’s physical location, combined with information about what advertisements you viewed and other information we collect, with our marketing partners.

- Demographic information

- Information regarding your use and navigation of our Services, such as clicks on pages, time spent on pages, scrolling, and other actions taken while using our Services

We, our vendors, and other third parties use information collected through tracking technologies:

- to ensure that the Services function properly

- to provide you with personalized location-based services and content

- to deliver personalized advertising on other online services, sites and apps and track responses to those advertisements

- to conduct analysis of the Services

- to combine information collected through tracking technologies with other information we obtain about you, which may include data we obtain from external parties

### Your choices regarding tracking technologies

The Website provides you a tracking technology management tool that allows you to change your preferences as to some of the tracking technologies that are enabled on the Website. To set or modify your tracking technologies click the “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link in the footer of the Website. To effectively manage cookies via this tool, you must set cookie preferences on all web browsers that you use. If you clear the cookies on your web browser(s) or device(s), you may need to set your cookie preferences again.

In addition, here is more information on how you may take steps to manage your preferences regarding tracking technologies using tools that we do not provide:

- Browser Settings. You can manage certain tracking technologies through your browser. At this time, like many other websites, the Website does not receive browser “Do Not Track” signals.

- Self-Regulatory Advertising Organizations. The Network Advertising Initiative and the Digital Advertising Alliance offer means to opt-out of certain advertising cookies through browsers and mobile apps.”

-
Please be aware that even if you are able to opt out of certain kinds of targeted advertising or tracking technologies through these tools, you may continue to receive other types of advertising, including advertising not tailored to your interest or advertising in other contexts.

Removing or blocking tracking technologies can impact your user experience and some functionality may no longer be available. These choices may be browser or device-specific, so if you use more than one browser or device, it may be necessary to make these choices in each of them to ensure your preferences are accounted.
