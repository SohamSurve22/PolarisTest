# CHS Global Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 08, 2025

This Privacy Notice covers both CHS online and offline data collection activities, including personal information that we collect through various channels such as websites, applications, contracts, points of sale, and events. As used in this Privacy Notice, CHS includes CHS Inc., CHS Hedging, LLC., CHS Market Advisors, LLC., as well as subsidiaries and affiliates.

If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Notice, please do not use CHS services or provide any personal information. If you decline to provide certain information when requested, CHS may not be able to provide the services for which you have contracted or requested.

## What’s Covered by this Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice covers any CHS-affiliated website, product, service, or communication that links to it and any collection, storage, transfer, sharing, disclosure or handling of your personal information when you engage with CHS.

This Privacy Notice does not cover, and CHS is not responsible for, third-party websites or applications that may be linked from CHS affiliated websites; linking to them does not imply an endorsement. CHS is not responsible for these websites or applications or any information they may collect. You should always review the privacy notice of any third-party website or application.

## What Personal Information CHS Collects

The following sensitive and non-sensitive personal information may be collected by CHS, depending on the services you requested:

- Identifiers which may include your real name, alias, contact information, video and image, IP address, online identifier, information sent to us by your web browser, email address, account name, website password and username, account registration information, usage data or other similar identifiers

- Background information which may include your professional associations, certifications, work history, and education history

- Financial Information which may include your credit card number, debit card number, corporate card numbers, income, transactions, credit information, taxation information, and other similar financial information

- Demographic information which may include your age, date of birth, race, color, ancestry, national origin, citizenship, religion or creed, marital status, sex, veteran or military status

- Health related information which may include medical conditions, physical or mental disability, genetic information (including familial genetic information), pregnancy or childbirth and related medical conditions

- Commercial information which may include voice recordings and data related to your purchasing, ownership percentage in business, consumer history, personal property and assets utilized for the consideration of a request to purchase products or services

- Information collected from or in connection with credit, know-your-client and other background verifications, including types and amounts of credit, payment history, negative banking items, collection actions, legal proceedings, previous bankruptcy and other credit-related information

- Information collected from or in connection with product/service orders, including proof of income (such as pay stubs or notices of assessment), bank account information, employment details (such as occupation and current or former employers)

- Information provided from or in connection with complaints, inquiries, surveys and customer research

- Information provided to or in connection with your interaction(s) or communication(s) with CHS employees

## How CHS uses Personal Information

CHS may retrieve, aggregate, alter or otherwise process your personal information with or without automated means when one or more of the following conditions exist:

- The personal information is provided voluntarily by you or with your consent to its processing. Should you wish to withdraw your consent at any time, contact the CHS Privacy Office.

- The processing is necessary for the performance of a contract with you or in order to take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract.

- The processing is necessary for credit services, including but not limited to, credit protection.

- The processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests which may include to:

- Maintain books and records

- Maintain the security and integrity of affiliated websites and systems

- Enforce the terms and conditions of the Privacy Notice associated with affiliated websites and systems

- Monitor the use and performance of affiliated websites and systems

- Provide quality assurance of customer service operations

- Make product improvements

- Make product developments

- Maintain health and safety

- Continue providing the same or similar products and services during internal or external organizational changes.

- The processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation.

- The processing is necessary in order to protect your vital interest or the vital interests of others.

- The processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest.

CHS may transfer your personal information to worldwide CHS subsidiaries and affiliates, service providers, non-affiliated partners, and joint-venture companies.

- Integrate data from multiple sources for analytics and reporting

- Track products in storage and product delivery via trucks, trains and barges

- Support general accounting purposes (accounts receivable, accounts payable, general ledger) as well as to invoice and process payments for transactions with third parties and required government reporting

- Track and report on workplace illnesses and injuries, for the protection of life, health, and the safety of property, and maintain crisis management plans

- Trade on the financial markets and provide market commentary to subscribers

- Provide remote IT support and workflow management between CHS systems

- Support enterprise-wide business activities and operations specifically as they relate to problem resolution, enterprise change management, ongoing hardware and software inventory, and to provide analytics related to customer service/satisfaction

- Provide office automation tools and other functions that aid and support the global CHS staff to optimized work activities

- Provide secure access to systems and networks

- Monitor network activity and system usage activity to maintain the confidentiality, integrity and availability (“CIA”) of personal data

- Document, track and respond to company legal and compliance investigations, lawsuits, and compliance requirements

- Provide relevant information about CHS products and services based on individuals signing up to receive such information

- Provide physical protection of personnel, hardware, software, networks and data from unauthorized physical access and video surveillance

- Procure indirect goods and services for CHS

- Perform due diligence on third parties that CHS may do business with and to conduct business continuity planning

- Fulfill legal obligations as required by government and regulatory agencies

- Support the proposed or actual purchase, sale (including a liquidation, realization, foreclosure or repossession, or sale at bankruptcy), lease, merger, amalgamation or any other type of acquisition, disposal, transfer, conveyance or financing of all or any portion of its business or of any assets or shares of CHS or a division
