## What Information We Collect and How We Collect It

We collect the following categories of information that you voluntarily provide to us when you communicate with Eversource or Eversource contractors or service providers. We collect only necessary
information that we use to provide you with our services, maintain your account, operate our business, and to otherwise communicate with you. We may share this information to service providers and contractors, regulatory authorities, and financial services entities or to other third parties as required by law.

Identifiers: Personal data such as your name, signature, certain Sensitive Personal Information elements, utility account number and service address, billing address, email address, account username, password, IP address, phone number(s), or other similar unique identifiers.

Sensitive Personal Information: We or our service providers may collect information such as your immigration status if you provide us with such information, biometric data in the form of voice authentication, if you consent and use our Interactive Voice Response System (IVR), Social Security Number, passport number, driver's license or other government issued ID number, financial account information, financial account log-in information or credit card or debit card number that in combination with any required access or security code, password or credential would allow access to such accounts, as well as health information that we receive from you or your health care providers at your request.

Account History and Payment Information: We or our service providers may collect your bank account, credit card or debit card information, and account history.

Demographic Data: Such as date of birth, household composition information and income.

Details About Your Home: Such as home ownership or renter status, dwelling age, size and type, homeowner information, and details about appliances and other energy-related equipment and systems used in the home.

Energy Usage and Energy Efficiency Information: Such as energy usage and information about your participation in our renewable energy, energy efficiency, or energy conservation programs, personal property, products or services obtained or considered, or other information related to your utility account history.

Geolocation Data: We collect precise geolocation information to provide you with our services and to better tailor our services to you depending on your geographical region.

Professional Information: Including information relating to your role as a representative or agent of a company or business, such as your work title and contact information.

Aggregated/De-Identified Information: We also collect information in an aggregated and de-identified form from various sources, including but not limited to accounts, transactions and usage. Such information does not identify individual customers but may be used for our business purposes – to identify new offerings, communications, research or analyze market trends, and other purposes consistent with applicable laws.

Online Information: We and our contractors and service providers also collect information you provide through your online activity, access and use of our and their websites and applications using tracking technologies such as cookies, Adobe Analytics, Google Analytics, pixel tags, web beacons, and clear GIFs, and through HTML-formatted email messages to measure the effectiveness and
success of our communications. Examples of the information that we collect through these tracking technologies include browsing history, search history, information about your interactions with our website, use of our mobile apps, or interactions with our digital advertisements, log data, including your Internet Provider (IP) address, a number that is automatically assigned to the device that you are using by your Internet Service Provider (ISP) is identified and logged automatically in our server log files whenever you visit our websites;  your Media Access Control (MAC) address, device type, screen resolution, operating system version and internet browser type
and version, time spent on our websites, pages you visited, location and your relationship with us, and statistical  information about your usage of our websites and applications. This online information is used to improve your online experience and is kept for a limited time period. All such information is deleted within 24 months of collection.

Third-Party Widgets:  We may allow certain widgets (e.g., social share buttons) on our sites and the sites of service providers and contractors that enable users to easily share information on another platform, such as a social media platform.  The third parties that own these widgets may have access to information about your browsing on the pages of our sites where these widgets are placed.  We recommend that you review the privacy and security policies and other terms and conditions of these third-party sites.

## How Do We Use Information We Collect?

Eversource may use your personal data for legitimate business purposes such as providing appropriate customer service, conducting business operations, complying with law, legal process or regulators, protecting the security of critical infrastructure and key resources, and ensuring information security. We do not use or disclose your personal data for any purpose other than as permitted by applicable law. We do not sell your personal data for monetary consideration. Eversource will also obtain your consent if it collects or processes your personal data for other purposes not contained in this Privacy Policy or otherwise disclosed to you.

We or our service providers may use the information we obtain about you to:

- Provide energy services or products to you.

- Communicate with you regarding our services and other products or services that you have requested or may be of interest to you.

- We may use household income and other information we collect to qualify you for low-income programs and reduced utility rates.

- Promote energy efficiency and to better understand your use of our services and provide you with information to improve your energy efficiency.

- Operate and maintain safe, secure and reliable gas and electric services and to serve you as a regulated public utility.

- Improve the functionality, design, security and usability of our websites and mobile applications, by customizing the content and layout of our pages for your use, by analyzing the statistical information that we collect about your usage of our websites to enable us to improve the design and functionality, to monitor responses of our communications and content, and to assist us with resolving customer questions regarding our websites.

- Improve our websites by customizing the content and layout of our pages for your use, by analyzing the statistical information that we collect about your usage of our websites to enable us to improve the design and functionality, to monitor responses
of our communications and content, and to assist us with resolving customer questions regarding our websites.

- Comply with a valid warrant, subpoena, or court order, or exercise or defend legal claims or otherwise comply with our legal obligations.

- Comply with regulatory requests or orders, or requests or orders from other local, state or federal governmental agencies with legal authority to obtain the personal data from Eversource.

- Develop and implement marketing, education and outreach plans to improve the services and products we provide to you and other consumers.

- Protect against and prevent fraud and identity theft, unauthorized transactions, claims and other liabilities, including past-due accounts.

- Enable service providers and contractors to provide energy-related services to you, products or equipment for Eversource business purposes consistent with Eversource's relationship with you and/or to comply with regulatory and legal obligations, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security requirements.

- Notify credit reporting agencies and collection agencies if your account is assigned for collection.

- Assist emergency responders in situations of threats to life or property.

- Create aggregated or de-identified data for analytics purposes.

- Provide Low or Zero Emission Project participants opportunities and information regarding energy efficiency services and programs that are synergistic with their projects.

- Exchange personal information with government agencies to identify your eligibility for participation in our financial hardship programs, service protection, and discounted rates.

- Collect and use personal data for the purpose of training large language models for internal purposes, for the benefit of customers.

- Share your usage data with third-party service providers you authorize.

- Share your personal information, including financial hardship status with electric suppliers to qualify you for reduced electric supply rates to comply with applicable laws.

- For any other business or lawful purpose reasonably anticipated within the context of Eversource's relationship with you.

- For any purpose for which we obtained your consent.

We retain your personal data based on legal requirements or business needs. Generally, we only retain personal data for as long as is reasonably necessary for our business purpose or as required by law.

## How Do We Share Your Information?

## How We Safeguard Your Information

We have rigorous security measures in place to protect and prevent the loss, misuse and alteration of the information that you provide to Eversource that is under our control. These measures may include device safeguards, industry-standard encryption technologies, secured files and buildings.

To protect your information, especially personal data, from unauthorized access and use, we use security measures that comply with applicable federal and state laws, as well as governmental and quasi-governmental agencies with regulatory authority over Eversource. We protect the confidentiality of the personal data we collect by maintaining physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards, with additional security measures and precautions for highly sensitive personal data such as Social Security Numbers and financial account numbers.

The measures we use to safeguard your information, particularly personal data, include:

- Information technology and physical security measures

- Limiting access to personal data

- Prohibiting unlawful disclosure of personal data

- Training our employees in the proper handling of personal data

- Requiring that third parties, such as service providers and contractors, that receive or have access to personal data, comply with all applicable laws and secure, have appropriate security controls and keep confidential and limit access to and use of your personal data

- Reviewing our safeguards on a regular basis

Please note that you will not be able to use an external virtual private network (VPN) to access account information for security reasons, as VPN prevents account verification.

## How You Can Safeguard Your Information

As an important security measure, you should never share your User ID or password with anyone else, and always be sure to log out and close your browser window when you have finished working to ensure that no one else can access your information. This is especially important if you share a computer with someone else or are using a computer in a library or other public place.

Eversource will never ask you for your password in an unsolicited phone call or unsolicited email.

It is important to note that email is not considered a secure medium and the transmission, receipt or security of information which you send via email cannot be guaranteed. Our email communications to you are available to anyone with access to your email account.

Unfortunately, no data transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100 percent secure. Although we use the best available technology (i.e., encryption, firewalls, access control software, etc.) to protect your information, we cannot guarantee the security of any information you transmit to us through our online products or services. As such, you provide information at your own risk. Once we receive your transmission, we employ rigorous security measures to protect against the loss, misuse and alteration of information on our systems.

You may opt out of providing certain types of information by not providing it or by discontinuing your use of the websites and applications; by setting preferences on your online account or by contacting us. Please note that even if you discontinue use of online account services, we will still store some personal data in our customer systems.

Exercising Your Privacy Rights – Authentication:

When you submit a request, we will take steps to verify your identity. You are not required to create an account with us to submit a request. We may ask you to provide us with information about yourself in order to verify your identity. We will only use the personal information you provide in connection with identity verification to verify the requestor's identity or authority to make the request and whenever feasible, we will attempt to match the information you provide with information we may already have about you.

You can use our Data Privacy Request form to exercise your rights.

In addition to the personal data described above, with your consent, provided directly to Eversource or through third parties, we may receive some information that may include sensitive personal data, which is defined by the CTDPA to include: (A) data revealing racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, mental or physical health condition or diagnosis, disability or treatment, sex life, sexual orientation or status as nonbinary or transgender or citizenship or immigration status, (B) consumer health data, (C) genetic or biometric data or information derived therefrom, for the purpose of uniquely identifying an individual, (D) personal data collected from a known child with parental or guardian authorization and consent, or (E) precise geolocation data (other than data generated by or connected to advanced utility metering infrastructure systems or equipment used by Eversource) (F) neural data, (G) a consumer's financial account number, financial account log-in information or credit card or debit card number that, in combination with any required access or security code, password or credential, would allow access to a consumer's financial account, or (H) government-issued identification number, including, but not limited to, Social Security number, passport number, state identification card number or driver's license number, that applicable law does not require to be publicly displayed. Please note that the type of sensitive personal data that we currently collect is limited and described above in What Information We Collect and How We Collect it – Sensitive Personal Information. You have the right to consent or revoke your consent to use of your sensitive personal data and to exercise your rights. You can use our Data Privacy Request form to exercise your rights.
