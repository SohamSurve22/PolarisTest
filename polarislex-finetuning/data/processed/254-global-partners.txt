- COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGY

We use different kinds of technology tools to recognize you, your browser(s) and/or your device(s) when you use the Services and to collect personal data about you when you interact with the Services. These tools include cookies, tracking pixels (sometimes called web beacons) and embedded scripts, which we refer to collectively as tracking technology.

Cookies are small text files that are sent to or accessed from your web browser or your computer’s hard drive. A cookie typically stores the name of the domain (internet location) from which the cookie originated, the “lifetime” of the cookie (i.e., when it expires) and a randomly generated unique number or similar identifier. Pixels are snippets of computer code embedded on websites that can collect information.

Tracking technology collects data related to usage and performance of the Global Consumer Websites, Global Apps and the Site, such as:

- IP address and time zone

- browser type and language

- computer or mobile device type and operating system

- device identifiers – IMEI/UDID (a numeric identifier for mobile devices) and MAC address (another kind of numeric identifier for mobile devices)

- user settings

- browsing history and activities conducted while using the Services

- websites visited before and after the Services

- Precise Geolocation Data (with consent)

Tracking technology enables Global to monitor and analyze usage, technical and navigational metrics; customize user experience; set or communicate with cookies; understand who visits the Services after seeing an online advertisement displayed on a third-party website; measure the success of email marketing campaigns; improve performance of the Services; and to help detect and prevent fraudulent use of the Services.

If you want to learn more about tracking technology, many online resources are available, such as www.allaboutcookies.org.

Cookies. The Services use the following cookies:

- Required cookies, which are required for the operation of the core functionality of the Services. Without them, for example, you would not be able to register for the Services that we offer.

- Functionality cookies, which are used to analyze use of the Services so that we can count the number of users, learn how users navigate the Services and us to improve the Services’ functioning.

- Advertising cookies, which are used to build profiles of your interests and personalize advertising relevant to your interests. We also may share this information with third parties to send you relevant advertising.

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies used in the Services. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is set. (Most browsers accept cookies automatically but allow you to disable them but note that some features of the Services may not work properly without cookies.) You also may be able to reject device identifiers by activating the appropriate setting on your mobile device.

Some web browsers (including Safari, Internet Explorer, Firefox, and Chrome) also have a “Do Not Track” (DNT) feature that tells a website that the user of that website does not want to have his or her online activity tracked. If a website that responds to a DNT signal receives a DNT signal, the browser can block that website from collecting certain information about the browser’s user.

The Services use Google Analytics for analytics and targeted advertising (which Google sometimes refers to as ‘remarketing’). Google uses cookies that recognize visitors to various websites. The data collected through Google’s cookies primarily helps Google create reports for Global about when and how the Services are used, such as which parts of the Services are most popular and which users return to the Services. Although Google Analytics sets a persistent cookie (i.e., the cookie persists until you delete it) in your web browser to identify you as a unique user of the Services, that persistent cookie is used and useable only by Google.

Google’s cookies also enable Global to personalize advertising. More information about how Google processes personal data collected via the Services is available at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. For more information about Google’s privacy practices, please visit Google’s Privacy Policy and for more information about how Google uses cookies in advertising, please visit Google’s Advertising page.

As of the “Last Updated” date above, you may disable Google’s cookies or delete any individual cookie. Google Analytics supports an optional browser add-on that – once installed and enabled – disables measurement by Google Analytics for any site you visit. Users also can install Google’s opt-out browser add-on. To opt out of targeted ads made available by Google, please use your Google Ads settings

Global Privacy Control (GPC): Some of the state privacy laws require Global to process GPC signals, which are signals sent by a platform, technology, or mechanism, enabled by individuals on their devices or browsers, that communicate the individual’s choice to opt-out of the sale of their personal data or use of their personal data for targeted advertising purposes. (Targeted advertising is advertising based on data collected about your activities over time and across other websites and services and used to infer or predict your interests or preferences.) To use an GPC, you can download a compatible internet browser or a plugin for on your current internet browser and follow the settings to enable to GPC. In states where this is required, we have configured both our consent management platform and our websites to receive and process GPC signals. We process GPC signals with respect to tracking technology, as discussed above and apply it to the specific browser on which you enable GPC. We do not charge your experience with the Services if you use GPC, although it may affect your use of certain aspects of the Services.

To opt-out of personalized advertising generally, visit DAA Opt-Out Page (web browser only) or AppChoices (mobile apps).

Opting out does not mean you will not see advertising – it means you will not see personalized advertising from the companies that participate in the opt-out programs. Also, if you clear your cookies, you many need to opt-out again.

- CONSUMER PRIVACY RIGHTS AND HOW TO EXERCISE THEM

Privacy Rights

The states in which Global offers the Services that have privacy laws in force (which are Connecticut, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Rhode Island, Texas, and Virginia) provide individuals with certain rights with respect to their personal data. Global will honor these rights in compliance with these laws.

The specific privacy rights available for consumers include:

- Right to Request Access: You have the right to know and access the personal data that Global processes about you.

- Right to Request Correction: You have the right to request that Global correct inaccuracies in your personal data that Global holds.

- Right to Request Deletion: You have the right to request the deletion of personal data provided by, obtained about or concerning you.

- Right Data Portability Request: You have the right to request a copy of your personal data processed by Global in a common, portable format. The personal data that you will receive in response to a valid data portability request varies in the states in which Global operates its Services for consumers.

- Right to Request a List of the Third Parties to Which Global Sells Your Personal Data. You may request a list of the specific third parties to which your personal data has been or may be sold. In some states in which Global operates its Services for consumers, “sold” means an exchange of personal data for monetary consideration, which Global does not do. In other states in which Global operates its Services for consumers, “sold” means exchange of personal data for monetary or other valuable consideration, which Global does do. You also may view the list here.

- Right to Opt-Out of Targeted Advertising: You have the right to opt out of Global’s targeted advertising using your personal data. (Targeted advertising is advertising based on data collected about your activities over time and across other websites and services and used to infer or predict your interests or preferences.)

- Right to Opt-Out of Sale: Where applicable, Global will allow you to opt out of the sale of your personal data. In some states in which Global operates its Services for consumers, “sale” means an exchange personal data for monetary consideration, which Global does not do. In other states in which Global operates its Services for consumers, “sale” means exchange of personal data for monetary or other valuable consideration, which Global may do.

The privacy rights described above are not absolute – exceptions and exemptions apply. If an exception or exemption applies to a privacy right request, Global will explain in a written response to your request.

As of the “Last Updated” date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy, Global does not (i) sell personal data for monetary consideration or (ii) conduct profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you (significant effect profiling). Accordingly, Global does not offer you the right to opt out of a sale of personal data for monetary consideration or the right to opt out of significant effect profiling.

If you do not reside in a state that offers privacy rights like the ones described above, Global may choose to honor your privacy rights as and when Global determines appropriate.
