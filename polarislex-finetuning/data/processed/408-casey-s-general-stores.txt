PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

We collect personal data from you through your use of the Site and Services. Personal data is information that is linked or reasonably linkable to an identified or identifiable individual. We collect the following types of personal data:

Personal Data as You Navigate Our Site

Depending on how you interact with our Site and Services, we will collect the following personal data that you voluntarily provide to us for the following purposes:

- Create an Account. If you create an account, you will provide us with your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, zip code, and a password for future login purposes. You may also provide us with a favorite store location or other preferences. If you use a third party’s sign in features to login to your account, please see the “Personal Data We Collect About You From Other Sources” section below.

- Place an Order. If you place an order, you will provide us with your name, email address, phone number, and financial information. To place a delivery order, you will also provide us with your physical address. We use third parties to process your payments. The third-party payment processor may collect, record, and store your payment information. Please review the third party’s privacy policy for more information. If you make an alcohol or tobacco purchase, then you will provide us with your driver’s license or date of birth and we will verify your age using a third party.

- Purchase a Gift Card. If you purchase a gift card, you will provide us with your name and financial information, as well as the recipient’s name and physical address. To notify the recipient of the purchase, you will provide us with their email address.

- Apply for a Credit Card. If you apply for a Casey’s Visa or Mastercard, you will provide your name, phone number, email address, date of birth, physical address, housing information, mother’s maiden name, social security number, citizenship status, employment status, and financial information. Visa and Mastercard will process this information and we recommend reviewing the parties’ privacy policies for more information.

- Apply for a Community Pizza Card. If you apply for a community pizza card, you will provide us with your name, physical address, phone number, email address, and signature. You will also provide us with the name of your non-profit organization.

- Sell Us Your C-Store. If you apply to sell us your convenience store, you will provide us with your name, title, physical address, phone number, email address. You will also provide us with your business’s name, physical address, number of locations, and any other information you choose to include.

- Contests, Sweepstakes, Promotions, and Loyalty Programs. When you sign up or participate in our loyalty program, Casey’s Rewards, or any type of contest, sweepstake, or promotion, you will provide us with your name, email address, date of birth, zip code, and phone number. Through the program, contest, sweepstake, or promotion, we may also collect your purchase history.

- Contact Us. If you contact us, you will provide us with your name, email address, phone number, and any other information you choose to include.

- Sign Up for Email. If you sign up to receive emails from us, you will provide us with your name and email address.

- Careers. If you apply for a job with us, you will provide us with your name, email address, physical address, phone number, current position, employer name, education information, employment history, and any other information you choose to include. Our careers page is provided by ADP. ADP may collect, record, and store the information you provide in your application. Please review ADP’s privacy policy here.

Information collected through automated means

When you interact with our Site, Services or Mobile App, we automatically collect certain information, including::

- Usage Information. For example, pages on the Site you access, the frequency of access, and what you click on while on the Site or App.

- Device Information. For example, hardware model, operating system, application version number, browser, and IP address (used for security, fraud prevention, and approximate location).

- Mobile Device Information. Aggregated information about whether the Site or App is accessed via a mobile device or tablet, the device type, and the carrier.

- Location Information. Location information from Site or app visits on a city-regional basis. Depending on your device’s settings, we may collect precise geolocation information. You can control our ability to do this by adjusting the location-sharing settings on your mobile device at any time.

For more information on our cookie usage see our “COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES” section below.

COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

Like many other companies, we use cookies and other tracking technologies (such as pixels and web beacons) (collectively, “Cookies”). “Cookies” are small files of information that are stored by your web browser software on your computer hard drive, mobile or other devices (e.g., smartphones or tablets).

We use Cookies to:

- Estimate audience size and usage patterns;

- Understand and save your preferences for future visits, allowing us to customize the Site and Services to your individual needs;

- Advertise new content and services that relate to your interests;

- Keep track of advertisements and search engine results;

- Compile aggregate data about site traffic and site interactions to resolve issues and offer better site experiences and tools in the future; and

- Recognize when you return to the Site.

We set some Cookies ourselves and others are set by other entities. We use Cookies set by other entities to provide us with useful information to help us improve our Site and Services, to conduct advertising, and to analyze the effectiveness of advertising. For example, we use Cookies from Google, Facebook and other similar companies.

How You Can Opt Out of Cookies

Cookies can be blocked by changing your Internet browser settings to refuse all or some Cookies. If you choose to block all Cookies (including essential Cookies) you may not be able to access all or parts of the Site.

You can find out more about Cookies and how to manage them by visiting www.AboutCookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.

Your use of Google reCAPTCHA is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. More information as to Google reCAPTCHA and how it works is available here.

UNIVERSAL OPT-OUT MECHANISMS

The Site recognizes the Global Privacy Control (“GPC”) signal. If you are using a browser setting or plug-in that sends an opt-out preference signal to each website you visit, we will treat that as a valid request to opt out. To download and use a browser supporting the GPC browser signal, click here: https://globalprivacycontrol.org/orgs. If you choose to use the GPC signal, you will need to turn it on for each supported browser or browser extension you use.

Some internet browsers incorporate a “Do Not Track” feature that signals to websites you visit that you do not want to have your online activity tracked. Given that there is not a uniform way that browsers communicate the “Do Not Track” signal, the Site does not currently interpret, respond to or alter its practices when it receives “Do Not Track” signals.
