## Personal Information We Collect

What is Personal Information?
We use the term “personal information” in this Privacy Policy to refer to information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated or linked, directly or indirectly, with you (or your household if you are a resident of California). Personal information also includes any information that can be combined with other information to identify, contact, or profile you.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to information that is: publicly available; aggregated, deidentified or anonymized; collected by third-party websites or social media platforms you reach by navigating from links on our Sites; or information about Lennar’s current and former employees or job applicants.

How We Collect Personal Information
We may collect your personal information in the following ways:

- Directly from you when you provide information to us or a Lennar Affiliate;

- Your communications with us by phone call, text message, e-mail, postal mail, via chat function or in-person at one of our Welcome Home Centers or properties;

- Your transactions with us or a Lennar Affiliate (with consent for marketing, as necessary to complete the transaction);

- From real estate, home products and services vendors, and other third-party companies with which we collaborate in home building, community development and other related services (“Business Partners”);

- From your visits to and interactions with our Sites collected via cookies and similar technologies placed by digital advertising networks, data analytics services, and social media (“Advertising Platforms”);

- From Advertising Platforms that collect and share information about the marketing and advertisements you have seen or clicked on;

- From other third party sources that provide consumer data, including internet browsing data, such as information about your interests, demographic information, and marketing inferences.

Categories of Information We Collect
The categories of personal information we collected and used for our business and commercial purposes during the past 12 months included the following:

- Identifiers (such as name, postal address, email address, social security number, driver’s license number, passport number, internet protocol address, device identifier, online account name and other online identifiers).

- Protected demographic data including age, marital status and military/veteran status (all information collected is used, or not used, in conformity with the Fair Housing Act.

- Consumer information (such as signature, telephone number, credit card, debit card, bank account and other financial information, health and other insurance information).

- Commercial information (such as credit reports, Lennar and Lennar Affiliate transaction history).

- Internet activity, including browsing history, search history, and a user’s interaction with a website, application, or advertisement (“Internet Usage Data”).

- Audio/visual data collected on Lennar-owned properties for security purposes. We record customer service calls for quality assurance and training.

Customer profile records that combine information and draw inferences from various sources about, for example, your transactions, home preferences, communications such as the content of emails, text messages, interactions with automated customer service features (such as a chatbot), and history of experiences with us.

Sensitive Information
Some of the personal information we collect is classified as sensitive information under the privacy laws of some states (“Sensitive Information”). For example, we may collect credit information to process your request to purchase a home, passport or visa information to establish citizenship or immigration status for legal purposes, or information for a personal accommodation related to your home design. When we request Sensitive Information, we will use it only for the purpose disclosed to you at or before the time the Sensitive Information was collected a purpose we reasonably believe is compatible with and expected by you based on the original disclosed purpose, or as required or authorized by law. Unless we request Sensitive Information for a specific purpose, we ask that you not send or disclose any Sensitive Information to Lennar. The types of Sensitive Information we may collect for business purposes include social security numbers, driver’s license, passport, and other government identification numbers; citizenship or immigration status; very limited medical information, and precise geolocation data.

## How We Use Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies

Cookies and Similar Technologies. Internet tracking and data storage technologies, commonly referred to as “cookies,” are placed on your device and stored on your browser when you visit our Sites. These technologies may include, for example, cookies, pixels, local device storage, web beacons, scripts, and device identifiers. They can be classified by:

- Time span. A session cookie is temporary and will expire when you close your browser; a persistent cookie will remain on your device until it automatically expires after a specified time period (or sooner if you delete it);

- Source. Either the operator of the website you are visiting (first-party cookie), or an authorized third-party service or platform (third-party cookie) can place the cookie; and

- Purpose. As described below, we use cookies for the proper functioning of our website, to learn about usage of our Sites, and for advertising and marketing purposes.

Third-Party Cookies. The types of data collected by third-party cookies generally do not directly identify an individual user and the analytics data we receive about our Sites is aggregated and anonymous. However, the third-party platforms we use for analytics and advertising purposes are able to track browsing activity on a device by a unique identifier and combine data collected from cookies on our Sites with data they collect from other sources to build a user profile that may be linkable to an identifiable person. For information about setting your cookie preferences and restricting cookies, see the section titled “How You Can Restrict Cookies and Targeted Advertising.”
The Advertising Platforms and other third parties placing cookies on our Sites are responsible for personal information they use for purposes other than providing services for us. They are also responsible for explaining their privacy practices to you. Some information about commonly used third-party cookies may be found at:

- https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites for how Google processes data from websites for advertising and data analytics purposes

- https://policies.google.com/privacy for information about controls Google offers to manage the collection and use of your personal information

- https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policies/cookies/?entry_point=cookie_policy_redirect&entry=0 for information about how Meta and Facebook use cookies

Categories of Cookies. The cookies we (or our authorized third‑party providers) place on our Sites can be categorized as described below. You may restrict data collection using any type of cookies placed on your device or browser except for cookies that are strictly necessary as explained in “How to Restrict Cookies and Targeted Advertising.”

Strictly necessary cookies are required for the basic operation and performance of our Sites and cannot be switched off. These are session cookies that are erased when you close your browser.

Performance cookies are used to identify and verify you and your device when you log in to access our Sites by storing, for example, your password, device identifier, browser type, operating system, Internet Service Provider (“ISP”) and Internet Protocol (“IP”) addresses. These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic so we can collect insights like which pages are the most popular and see how visitors move around the site. You can restrict or delete these cookies as explained in “How to Restrict Cookies and Targeted Advertising.”

Analytics cookies let us know how you and other visitors interact with our Sites and which other websites you browse before and after visiting our Sites. These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic so we can identify which pages are the most popular and see how visitors move around the Sites. Google, Salesforce, Facebook, Hotjar, TikTok and other services provide website and advertising performance analysis by collecting data across many users’ devices and tracking users’ activities on our Sites and other websites and platforms. These cookies qualify as persistent cookies because they remain on your device or browser after you close the current session. You can restrict or delete these cookies as explained in “How to Restrict Cookies and Targeted Advertising.”

Targeted advertising cookies may be placed on your device or browser by us or our authorized Advertising Platforms, including (among others) the Google, Facebook, and Microsoft advertising platforms. The Internet Usage Data transmitted by these third-party cookies enables the advertising networks to build a profile of your preferences and interests and show you advertisements based on your internet activities over time and across many nonaffiliated websites, applications, and platforms you visit. The data may also be linked across the different devices you use. Targeted advertising cookies do not store directly identifying personal information, but they store data that may uniquely identify your browser and device. You can restrict or delete these cookies as explained in “How to Restrict Cookies and Targeted Advertising.”

Social media cookies, including the Meta (Facebook) pixel, , are placed by social media networks. They enable you to share content from our Sites through social media networks and help us to analyze the effectiveness of content and marketing we post on social media. These cookies collect, for example, web activity, IP address, device and browser characteristics, and timestamps of visits. These cookies help us to analyze the effectiveness of advertisements we post on social media. The social media networks also collect data about your activities on our Sites. For information about how social media networks use the data collected by cookies, and how to restrict their data collection, see the privacy and cookie policies issued by the social media platforms you use.

E-mail cookies may be used to give us information such as how you interact with our e-mail communications, including whether and when you opened it, what links you clicked within the e-mail, and if it was forwarded.
