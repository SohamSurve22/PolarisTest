### INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect various types of personal information provided by users of the Services. This may include such information as:

- Contact and Identifying Information: We may collect contact and identifying information, such as names, home addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, and in some instances Social Security numbers or health information, and other application or quote information.

- Application, Quote, or Claim: If you submit an application, quote, or a claim through our Services, we may collect information that you provide and other related information such as automobile information (including driver’s license number, vehicle identification number, and accident history).

- Account Information: If you create, register, or connect an online account in connection with our Services, we may store your username, password (and password hint information) and other basic account information that you may provide or that may be associated with that account.

- Job Application: If you apply for a job with us, we may collect information about your work and educational history, and other information that you choose to provide us in connection with your application.

- Information Collected Using Cookies, Pixels or Other Tracking Technologies: We and third-party online advertising and analytics companies may use cookies, pixels/web beacons, or similar tracking technologies to automatically collect information about your online activity, browser and device, as well as to collect information when you interact with the Services or other The Hartford offerings. In addition to the information above collected generally by the Services, unique device IDs, mobile phone numbers, geo-location data, or call logs may be collected through our mobile applications. See the “Cookies” and “Your Choices” section below for more information on these technologies and your choices.

- Online Activity Information: We and third-party online advertising and analytics companies may collect personal information relating to users of our Services over time and across different websites.

- Log Information: We also collect log information when you use the Services, which may include information about how you have used the Services, device information, access times, IP addresses, app usage data, general geolocation, demographic information, and information regarding your use and navigation of our Services. In some cases, we may use technologies such as session replay to record your interactions with the Services, including mouse movements, clicks on pages, page views, time spent on pages, scrolling, text entered and other actions taken while using our Services or behavioral data, as well as related browsing information.

### Text Messaging/SMS Policy

If you opt into our Text Messaging/SMS (“SMS”) program, we will send you messages regarding our services, updates, policies, and offers. We will also send messages for customer service and support purposes. By subscribing to our SMS program, you consent to receive SMS messages at the phone number you provided to us. You can opt-out at any time by replying “STOP” to any SMS message you receive from us or by contacting us at the email address or telephone number provided below. Your consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing our services or products. Message and data rates may apply.

We will not share your consent to receive SMS messages from us with third parties for their marketing purposes unless you have provided consent to receive SMS messages from that third party. However, we may share your information with third parties that help us provide SMS services, such as platform providers and phone companies. We do not permit third parties to use or sell the SMS consent we receive from you for any purpose other than providing services to us.

### COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

We or third-party online advertising and analytics companies may use tools and services to gather, analyze, and store information about users, including for statistical purposes, fraud prevention, security monitoring, session replay, reporting, attribution, analytics, market research, interest-based advertising, ad delivery, personalization, and other purposes described in this Privacy Policy. These cookies, web beacons, pixels, tags, scripts, session replay technologies, browser storage technologies, and similar technologies are used to help analyze how you use online services or to collect or receive information from our websites and elsewhere on the internet and use that information to provide measurement services and target ads. Generally speaking, cookies are small text files that are placed on your browser that can be used to identify you across web pages and sessions. Web beacons are small pieces of code that can communicate information about your device or browser to a server. Session replay technologies can record your interactions with our Services, including mouse movements, clicks, scrolling, and text entered to help us troubleshoot issues, analyze usage patterns, and improve performance.

For example, we use Facebook Business Tools. We also use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, Inc. (“Google”). Please use the following link to understand how Google Analytics collects and processes data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. To prevent the storage and processing of this data (including your IP address) by Google, you can download and install the browser plug-in available at the following link: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout?hl=en.
