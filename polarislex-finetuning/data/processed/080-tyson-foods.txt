# Tyson Foods Global Privacy Notice

February 28, 2026

This Privacy Notice applies to the websites, applications and platforms where it appears, as well as any other points of contact with Tyson Foods.

Maintaining the security of your data is a priority for Tyson Foods, Inc. and its subsidiaries, affiliates and related entities (collectively, "Tyson Foods," "we" or "us"). Tyson Foods is committed to being transparent about what data we collect about you and how we use it. This Privacy Notice applies to all users of our websites, applications, platforms and features (collectively, "Site(s)"), as well as all communication with us or participation in any of our programs or events. We may provide additional privacy notices in connection with certain activities, programs or offerings. We may also provide "just-in-time" notices that may supplement or clarify our privacy practices or provide you with additional choices regarding your personal information.

This Privacy Notice applies to Tyson Foods’s information practices for you to understand how personal data is collected through our Sites and how it is used, stored and disclosed. This Privacy Notice also describes your rights regarding the personal data we hold about you, including how you can exercise those rights.

We are committed to protecting the privacy of minors. To comply with various privacy regulations, at this time our Sites are not intended to be used by anyone under the age of 18 and are not designed to attract children under the age of 18. Anyone under the age of 18 using our Sites should only do so with participation of a parent or other guardian. If a parent or other guardian becomes aware that his or her child has provided us with information without their consent, the parent or other guardian should contact us at privacy@tyson.com. Tyson Foods will delete such information within a reasonable time.

This Privacy Notice may change from time to time. You should review this Privacy Notice periodically. We will bring any material changes to your attention by posting an updated version.

This Privacy Notice does not address concerns about Tyson Foods products. FOR CONSUMER RELATIONS OR TO CONTACT A REPRESENTATIVE REGARDING OUR PRODUCTS, please call 1-800-233-6332.

Who is the controller of your personal data?

Under certain privacy laws, the “Controller” or “Personal Information Processor” of your data is Tyson Foods, Inc. We will only process your personal data according to this Privacy Notice, unless otherwise required by applicable law. We take steps to ensure that the personal data that we collect about you is adequate, relevant, not excessive and processed for limited purposes.

What is personal data?

Personal data is information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, to an identified or identifiable living natural person or household. An ‘identifiable person’ is one who can be identified, directly or indirectly, in particular by reference to an identifier such as a name, an identification number, location data, an online identifier, or to one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural, or social identity of that natural person or other factors that can identify an individual.

Profiling

As part of certain promotions located within the United States, we may collect personal data about you (i.e. location data, product interest, preferences and other information) that you provide us when you create an account on our platforms. This section only applies when we use automated processing on your personal information to evaluate, analyze, or predict your personal preferences. We centralize this data and analyze it to evaluate and predict your personal preferences and/or interests. Based on our analysis, we send communications and/or content (i.e. digital advertising) tailored to your preferences.

When we send personalized communications or content, we may use some techniques qualified as “profiling” (i.e. any form of automated processing of personal data consisting of using those data to evaluate certain personal aspects relating to a natural person, in particular to analyze or predict aspects concerning that natural person’s personal preferences, interests, economic situation, behavior, location, health, reliability, or movements).

You may have the right to access and obtain communication about the decision based on this profiling, including the logic involved and the consequences. You can update and delete your information through your platform account at any time. You also have the right to object to the use of your data for “profiling” in certain circumstances. Please see "HOW CAN YOU EXERCISE YOUR RIGHTS REGARDING YOUR PERSONAL DATA?" section below.

Depending on your residency, these rights may not apply to pseudonymous data if the information necessary to identify the consumer is kept separately and is subject to controls that prevent access to the information. Pseudonymous data is personal information that can no longer be attributed to a specific individual without the use of additional information, if the additional information is kept separately and is subject measures to ensure that personal information is not attributed to the specific individual. section below.

Data solely retained for data backup or archive purposes is principally excluded from these rights until it is restored to an active system or next accessed or used for a sale, disclosure, or commercial purpose.

The disclosure of the value described herein is not intended to waive, nor should be interpreted as a waiver of, our proprietary or business confidential information, including trade secrets, and does not constitute any representation with regard to generally accepted accounting principles or financial accounting standards.
