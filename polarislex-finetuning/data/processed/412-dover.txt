A. Summary of Privacy Rights

California law provides Californians with the following rights and choices regarding their Personal Information.

(i) Correct

Californians have the right to request the correction of their inaccurate Personal Information.

(ii) Delete

Californians have the right to request the deletion of their Personal Information unless an exception applies, including but not limited to:

- The data is necessary to complete a transaction for that person.

- The data is necessary to fulfill a warranty or support a product recall.

- The data is necessary to provide goods or services requested by that person.

- The data is necessary to perform a contract between Dover and that person.

- The data is reasonably used within the context of the ongoing business relationship between Dover and that person.

- Dover uses the data to identify and repair issues with our Services.

- Dover must retain the data to comply with a legal obligation.

(iii) Do Not Sell/Share

If Dover sold or shared Personal Information, Californians have the right to opt-out of Dover's sale/sharing of their Personal Information.

(iv) Freedom from Discrimination

Dover may not discriminate against Californians who exercise their privacy rights.

(v) Limit

Californians may have the right to limit Dover’s use or disclosure of their Sensitive Personal Information as defined under California law if Sensitive Personal Information is used for purposes other than those expressly permitted by law.

(vi) Know

Californians have the right to know what Personal Information is or has been collected by Dover.
