CCTV (CLOSED CIRCUIT TELEVISION) DATA

Applied uses CCTV to: (i) manage building access and protect the security of Applied facilities, personnel, visitors, property, and information on the premises; (ii) prevent, deter, and, where necessary, investigate unauthorized physical access, including access to secure areas, protected rooms, IT infrastructure, or operational information; and (iii) prevent, detect, and investigate theft of Applied, visitor, or employee property, as well as threats to workplace safety, such as fire or physical assault. Applied processes CCTV data based on its legitimate interests as controller.

Applied does not use CCTV for unrelated purposes, such as monitoring employee work performance or attendance. The cameras are positioned to provide a general view of activity in designated areas and are not intended to monitor surrounding public spaces or identify individuals, except where necessary for security, safety, access control, incident response, or legal purposes. CCTV is not used as an investigative tool or to collect evidence for internal investigations or disciplinary procedures unless a security, safety, legal, or compliance incident is involved. In exceptional circumstances, CCTV data may be shared with investigatory authorities in connection with a formal disciplinary, regulatory, or criminal investigation. Cameras are installed at entrances and focused on individuals accessing Applied sites or related facilities, including parking areas. The CCTV system covers building entry and exit points, internal access points, delivery areas, garages, and exterior areas. The system captures images only; no audio is recorded. Images are not shared with third parties except as described above or as required by law, and access is limited to authorized personnel. CCTV images are periodically overwritten and deleted once the purposes for which they were collected have been fulfilled, unless longer retention is required for legal, compliance, security, or investigation purposes.

MEETING AND CALL RECORDINGS

When you participate in a virtual, telephone, or in-person meeting, call, webinar, or training session with Applied Materials (for example, using Microsoft Teams or a similar platform), we may record the session. Depending on the meeting and the settings enabled, a recording may capture audio, video, images, screen or other content shared during the session, chat messages, and a transcript or AI-generated notes or summary of the discussion. We may also collect related information such as participant names, email addresses, and the date, time, and duration of the session.

We use meeting recordings for legitimate business purposes, including to create a record of the meeting, to allow those who could not attend to review the discussion, for note-taking, quality, training, and internal reference, and to protect our rights and comply with our legal and regulatory obligations. The legal basis for this processing is Applied Materials’ legitimate interests in conducting and managing its business operations, your consent where required by applicable law, or the performance of or entry into a contract.

Where a session is being recorded, we will make this known to participants before or at the start of the session, for example through a recording notification within the meeting platform and/or a verbal or written announcement. If you do not wish to be recorded, you may decline to join, turn off your camera and microphone, or notify the host. Recordings are accessible only to those with a business need and are retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law. Where applicable law requires the consent of all participants to a recording, we will obtain that consent before recording. We do not use recordings to generate biometric identifiers such as voiceprints or facial-geometry scans. Where we use automated tools to produce transcripts, notes, or summaries of a session, the output is reviewed by a person before it is relied on for any decision affecting you.

YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your country/region of residency, you may have the following rights concerning your personal information (subject to legally permissible restrictions):

- Right of access: You may request access to your personal information, including requesting a copy thereof.

- Right to rectification: You may request to update or correct any inadequate, incomplete, or inaccurate personal information.

- Right to erasure: You may request that we erase your personal information, if such information is no longer necessary for the purposes it was collected, it was or is being processed unlawfully, or you have permissibly withdrawn your consent to such processing (where our processing is based on your consent).

- Right to restrict: You may request that we cease further processing of your personal information.

- Right to data portability: You may request that we provide your personal information in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format for your use or transfer.

- Right to object: You may object to Applied’s processing of your personal information for direct marketing purposes, for our own legitimate interest, or to allow us to perform a task in the public interest.

- Right to withdraw your consent: Where Applied is processing your personal information based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

- Right to lodge a complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority if you believe that Applied’s processing of your personal information violates data protection regulations.

- Rights in relation to automated decision-making: You have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you, except where permitted by applicable law. Where we carry out such processing, you may request meaningful information about the logic involved and the significance and envisaged consequences of the processing, express your point of view, and request human review of the decision.

- Right to non-retaliation: We will not discriminate or retaliate against you for exercising any of these rights.

If you want to exercise any of these rights or have any questions or concerns about how we treat your personal information, please email your request to DataProtection@amat.com.
