- Identifiers: We collect identifiers which include personal information like name, mailing address, email address, phone number, and shipping address and may also include information such as your social media account information.

- Transactional Information: Your payment details, such as payment card information, billing address, items purchased, and related transaction records can be collected in connection with an order or purchase.

- Call, Chat, Text, Email, and Mail Records: If you call, chat, text, email, or mail our automated or live customer service agents, we may record and retain those conversations, including any photographs or videos you may share with us.

- Commercial Information: We keep track of the services or products you purchase, your consuming histories or tendencies, and any membership and loyalty program participation information.

- Submitted Content: We collect any content you submit to the BJ's Sites, Social Media Pages, or to us including comments, photos, reviews, promotions, or sweepstakes entries.

- Unsubmitted Content: Information that you type into search bars or other text submission areas, even if you do not click submit.

- Demographic Information/Characteristics of protected classifications: We may obtain demographic details such as birthdate, gender, ZIP code, and other similar details.

- Education Information: We may collect certain information about your education in connection with programs we offer.

- Membership Information: We may collect membership number and additional information from you such as driver's license number to confirm your eligibility for certain types of memberships and your exercise of certain membership benefits (e.g., reward redemption or ability to purchase alcohol).

- Job Applications: If you apply for employment, we have access to the content you provide such as your resume, cover letter, and any information you provide to us when submitting an employment application form.

- Other Information You Provide: We may collect other information you provide to us, such as when contacting us or through the course of visiting or using BJ's Sites, BJ's WiFi, or our Clubs, or otherwise interacting with us, including engaging with BJ's Social Media Pages.

- Inferences: We may use any of the above information to draw inferences from these categories of information such as consumer preferences, behavior, and characteristics.

- Geolocation: We may collect your device’s approximate location. When you enable location services, we may also collect your precise location.

Note, as a membership-based wholesale club offering membership-only privileges, including certain loyalty-based offerings, if you choose not to provide us with the information requested, we may not be able to provide you with certain services, including your membership or any loyalty-based offerings.

Information Automatically Collected From You

We, and certain business partners and other third parties (such as market research companies, analytics providers, advertising partners, social media platforms, and technology providers), may automatically collect information from you or your devices while you visit or use BJ's Sites, BJ's WiFi, or our Clubs, by automated means such as cookies, pixel tags, server or device logs and other technologies. A “cookie” is a text file that websites send to a visitor’s device to uniquely identify the visitor’s browser or to store information or settings in the browser. A “pixel tag” (also known as a web beacon) is a type of technology that is placed on a website or within an email to track certain activity, such as views of a website or when an email is opened. These technologies help us (and if applicable our business partners and other third parties) (1) remember your information so you do not have to re-enter it; (2) track and understand how you use and interact with us; (3) tailor our services around your preferences; (4) measure the usability of the BJ's Sites, and the effectiveness of our communications; (5) authenticate your identity, protect against fraud and provide our products and services; and (6) otherwise manage and enhance our products and services, and help ensure they are working properly.

These automated technologies may be used to collect information about your device, browsing actions and usage patterns, such as your device IP address, general location information, unique device identifiers, device type and model, device characteristics and settings, browser settings and characteristics, operating system type and version, language and country preferences, usage statistics, referring emails and web addresses, and other device details. We, and certain business partners and other third parties, also may obtain information about your interactions with the BJ’s Sites, such as pages visited, links clicked, features used, dates and times of usage, session information and other information about your use of BJ’s Sites.

In addition, these automated technologies may collect your device’s geolocation information through GPS, Bluetooth, WiFi signals and other technologies. Your device’s operating platform may provide you with a notification when the app attempts to collect your precise geolocation. Please note that if you decline to allow the app to collect your precise geolocation, you may not be able to use all of the BJ’s Sites’ features or the offers available through the BJ’s Sites.
Your browser may tell you how to be notified about certain types of automated collection technologies and how to restrict or disable them. Please note, however, that without these technologies, you may not be able to use all of the features of the BJ’s Sites. To stop sharing your location on a mobile device, go to your device’s settings and turn off location permissions.

When you access BJ's WiFi, we may also automatically collect information from your mobile device, such as the device's Media Access Control ("MAC") address, its association to specific wireless access points (which can be used to approximate your location, including proximity to a BJ's Club), date, time and duration of use, device type and operating system, and the amount of bandwidth used.
