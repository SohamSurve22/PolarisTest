# Nordstrom Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 12, 2026

Using your personal information to provide you with the service you expect from Nordstrom.

At Nordstrom our customers are at the heart of what we do. We want to provide products and services that help you feel good and look your best and that includes giving you confidence in how we handle your personal information. We take the trust you place in usseriously, no matter whether you share personal information with us when visiting or making a purchase at our stores, reaching out to us by phone or email, using our websites ("Websites"), or our mobile applications ("Apps"). Within this policy, we will refer to the Websites, Apps, in-store activities, and communications with us collectively as the "Services." The goal of this Privacy Policy ("Policy") is to explain how Nordstrom, Inc., including our affiliates, subsidiaries, and family of brands (Nordstrom and Nordstrom Rack) (collectively, "Nordstrom", "we" or "us"), collect and use your personal information, who we disclose it to and when, and the tools and options available to you to manage that information.

This Policy applies to Nordstrom Services, and any sites, mobile apps or products that display or link to this Policy. It does not apply to any website, mobile app, service, or product that does not display or link to this Policy or that contains its own privacy policy. If you are the holder of a Nordstrom branded credit or debit card, please review the Nordstrom Debit Card Privacy Notice and Nordstrom Credit Card Privacy Notice for information about how Nordstrom Card Services and our banking partner use personal information associated with your Nordstrom branded credit or debit card.

What personal information we collect

These are the categories of personal information we may collect and how long we keep it:

- Identifiers like name, alias, phone number, mailing and email addresses, government-issued ID number, IP address, account name, account credentials, address book entries, birth date, ZIP code; we keep this while you are a customer and as long as necessary for legal reasons or to protect our interests.

- Commercial Information like purchase and transaction information, Nordstrom account log-in credentials and password, payment method(s), items purchased, items altered or custom made (including measurements), order status, wish list items, shopping bag items, registry items, product review information, gift card and Nordstrom Note information, preferred product sizes, brands you tell us you love, merchandise interests, Nordy Club member status, income range; we keep this while you are a customer and as long as necessary for legal reasons or to protect our interests.

- Information from your communications with us like the content of email messages, chat sessions, text messages, phone calls; we keep this for as long as we need the information to provide service to you.

- Demographic information like Age or birth date, gender, ZIP code, Nordy Club member status, other information about you; we keep this while you are a customer, and longer if required by law or to protect our interests.

- Audio and visual information like video recordings of you when you visit one of our stores, call recordings when you call Customer Care team; we keep this for one year or longer as necessary for legal reasons or to protect our interests.

- Location or geolocation information like device location information when using our Apps (if device settings permit); we keep this for one year or longer as necessary for legal reasons or to protect our interests.

- Internet or other electronic network activity information like internet browsing information, unique device identifiers, cookie identifiers, device and browser settings and information, interaction with Website, Apps or advertisement, Internet service provider information, date and time of visit or use, websites visited before and after, navigation and search on Websites or Apps, pages visited, items viewed, items purchased; we keep this while you are a customer, and longer if required by law or to protect our interests.

Inferences including inferences about or derived from any of the information above that may relate to your preferences or other matters; we keep this while you are a customer, and longer if required by law or to protect our interests.

Our Site is for individuals who are at least 13 years old

Nordstrom does not knowingly request or collect personal information from anyone younger than 13 years old (or other age required by law) without prior consent from a parent or legal guardian. Please contact privacy@nordstrom.com if you believe your child under age 13 has submitted personal information to Nordstrom without prior parental or legal guardian consent so that we can take steps to delete the information.

We collect personal information using automated technologies

When you visit our Websites or use our Apps, we automatically collect some information using technologies like cookies. Here's what you should know:

We automatically create website and mobile application log files, which are created when you access our Websites and/or Apps. In addition, automated data collection technologies—such as cookies, beacons, tags, software development kits (SDK), and scripts—are used by us to analyze trends, administer the Websites and Apps, and track users' movements around the Websites and Apps. We, and our third-party service providers, also use these technologies to gather demographic information about our user base as individuals and in the aggregate.

A 'cookie' is a small file stored on your device when you visit a website. Cookies help websites remember your preferences and recognize you on return visits. We use the following types of cookies:

- Essential Cookies are required to make our Websites and Apps function properly and cannot be turned off in our systems. If you block these cookies, portions of our Websites or Apps may not function properly.

- Performance and Functional Cookies allow us to count visits and sources of traffic on our Websites and Apps so we can measure and improve performance and functionality. They help us know which pages of our Websites are most and least popular, which features of the Apps are used most, and how visitors move around the Websites and Apps.

- Targeted Advertising Cookies may be set through our Websites by our advertising partners. They may be used by advertising companies to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant advertisements on other websites. Please see Targeted Advertising in the Your options and legal disclosures  section for information about how you can opt out of these cookies.

- Social Media Cookies allow you to share information using social media sharing buttons on social networks like Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Instagram, or Twitter. This information may then be used by the social media network to deliver targeted advertising to you.

We may combine information collected automatically (as described above) from your interactions with our Services and your activity on third-party sites with other data (including personal information).

If you wish to opt out of allowing certain cookies, you may do so by changing your browser settings so that cookies from our Websites and Apps cannot be set (see Targeted Advertising in the Your options and legal disclosures section below for your options). Please note that if you delete, block, or otherwise restrict cookies, or use a different computer or Internet browser, you will need to renew your cookie management choices.

- Updating your personal information. You can also correct or update your name, email, billing or shipping addresses, some shopping preferences (such as preferred store location) and payment methods by logging into your account on the Websites. You may also make a request to update your personal information by writing to us at the email or postal mailing address specified in the Contact us section below.

To adjust settings for Nordstrom and Nordstrom Rack, please go to our opt-out page. Our Canadian customers may make purchases through U.S. Direct E-Commerce Limited ("ESW"), or may shop in one of our stores in the U.S. When you make a purchase through ESW, they will process your personal information to fulfill and deliver your order and share information about your transaction with us. Canadian privacy laws provide specific rights to our customers located in Canada. If you are a Canadian resident and a Nordstrom customer, this section applies in addition to all other applicable rights and information contained in this Policy.

Canadian privacy law provides Canadian residents the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information. To submit a request for access, correction or deletion of your personal information, you may complete our online form or write to us at the email or postal mail address in the Contact us section below.

Identity verificationrequirement . Before we honor a personal information request from a customer residing in Canada, we validate the identity associated with the request. As a result, we may require that you take additional steps to validate your identity or legal authority, particularly where you make a request through our online form or in writing (email or postal mail). If you make a request to access, correct or delete your personal information, we will send a message to the email address you provided in your request and you must follow the instructions in that email for us to verify your request. We will begin processing your request once you have completed the verification process.

We will provide a response to any personal information request within the timeframe required by law. If we cannot substantively respond to your request in a timely manner, we will notify you and provide the reason for the delay.

Under certain circumstances, we may not be able to fulfill your request, such as when doing so would interfere with our regulatory or legal obligations, where we cannot verify your identity, or if your request involves disproportionate cost or effort. But we will respond to your request within a reasonable time, as required by law, and provide an explanation.
