YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

This section describes certain rights that are available to you under your state law and explains how to exercise those rights. Note that the specific rights that may be available to you may depend on the state law where you are a resident.

- Request to Confirm Processing- You have the right to confirm whether we are processing your personal information.

- Request to Know - You have the right to request that we disclose certain information to you about our collection and use of your personal information and a right to request the specific pieces of personal information we collected about you (also called a data access request). This Request to Know covers:

- the categories of personal information that we have collected about you;

- the categories of sources from which your personal information is collected;

- the business or commercial purpose for collecting, or “sharing” for online targeted advertising, your personal information;

- the categories of personal information that we have disclosed, “shared” for online targeted advertising, and the categories of third parties with whom we have disclosed your personal information or “shared” it for online targeted advertising.

- Data Portability – You have a right to request a copy of personal information that we maintain about you, where technically feasible and in readily usable format that allows you to easily transmit to another company. This right however may be subject to certain exceptions.

- Request List of Third-Party Recipients - In some states, you may have the right to obtain a list of specific third parties to which we have disclosed personal information. For a list of the specific third parties to whom we have disclosed personal information, please submit a request via the Data Subject Request portal linked below. Please note, some areas also allow you to obtain a list of the categories of third parties to which we have disclosed personal information. You can find that information in the table above under the “How We Disclose, Sell or Share Personal Information” section.

- Request to Delete - You have the right to request that we delete any of your personal information that we collected from you and retained, subject to certain exceptions.

- Request to Correct - You may request that we correct any inaccurate personal information from our records.

- Request to Opt-Out of Sharing for Targeted Advertising - You may have the right to request to opt-out of the sharing for targeted advertising of personal information (if applicable). Please note that after you opt-out of receiving targeted advertisements, your information may still be collected through cookies on our websites for certain purposes not related to targeted advertising. We recognize universal opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control (GPC) that permits users to exercise opt-out of sharing their Personal Information for online targeted advertising. Please be aware that, opt out preference signals may be used and recognized only by certain website browsers or devices that support capabilities. For more information on how we use data collection technologies, including how to change or control your preferences, please refer to our Cookies and Other Technologies Policy.

- Opting-Out of E-mails or Text Messages - If you have signed up to receive marketing or promotional emails from Biogen about our products, and you no longer wish to receive these emails, you can unsubscribe by clicking on the “Unsubscribe” link provided within the e-mail, or submit a request to our Data Subject Request portal linked below. You can also send an e-mail with the word “Unsubscribe” to privacy@biogen.com to initiate your request. You may opt out of text messages by following instructions in the text message.

- Right to Non-Discrimination - You have a right not to be subject to discriminatory treatment for the exercise of your privacy rights.

EXERCISING YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

To exercise the rights described above, please submit a request to us online at our Data Subject Request portal, contact us toll-free at 1-877-318-6245, or email us at privacy@biogen.com.

When you submit a request to us, you will be asked to affirm your state of residency and to indicate whether the request is on your behalf or for someone else. If you make a request related to Personal Information about you, we will need to verify your identity. For instance, we may request that you match specific pieces of information you have provided us, or provide a power of attorney or a signed declaration that you are the individual whose personal information is the subject of the request. For requests related to particularly sensitive information, we may require additional proof of identification. If you make a request through an authorized agent, we will require written proof that the agent is authorized to act on your behalf. We will use any information that we request to verify your identity only for these purposes.

We will process your request within the time provided by applicable law. If we are not able to comply with your request, you will be provided with the reasons for such decisions. You may be entitled to appeal our decisions and can do so by following the instructions provided in the correspondence we send to you communicating our decision. You may also be entitled to lodge a complaint with regulators.
