Privacy principles

Our Privacy Policy requires us to adhere to these privacy principles, meaning you can count on us to protect and secure your information.

- We only process personal data needed to do our work.

- We limit the use of personal data to only the original purposes it was collected.

- We use personal data in a lawful, honest and fair way.

- We keep personal data accurate and up-to-date.

- We retain personal data for only as long as it is needed.

- We maintain the security of personal data.

- We respect every person’s privacy rights.

- We are accountable for the personal data we use and share.
