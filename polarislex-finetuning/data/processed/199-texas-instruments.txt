Separate or additional terms may apply where specifically stated for tools, mobile applications, social computing environments or third-party sites.

If you do not agree with the practices described in the current version of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use our sites or engage with us.

### 1. Information we collect

When you visit our sites, indicate interest in our products and/or services, establish or maintain a business relationship, and/or otherwise interact with us, TI collects a broad variety of information relating to you as an identified or identifiable person (“Personal Data”). Personal Data may include, for example, email addresses, names, or phone numbers. TI may collect and use your Personal Data consistent with this Privacy Policy. You are not under any contractual nor legal obligation to provide us with your Personal Data. However, if we cannot collect this information, we may not be able to provide you (or the company you work for or represent) with products or services, or enter into a respective contract, and/or you (or the company you work for or represent) may not be able to take advantage of certain features and programs.

Categories of the information we may collect and store include:

- Webpages you have visited (for example, whether you look at support forums, or product information);

- Information transmitted by the device used to access the sites (technical information such as operating system, device identifier, IP address, language and geolocation, etc.), including information obtained by cookies and other tracking technologies used on our sites (for more information regarding cookies and tracking technologies used on our sites, please review our Cookie Policy below);

- Any information that you post or submit (for example, where you request more information, register for an event, or post information or a request in forums on the sites), including identity verification data (such as ID/passport information or other proof of identity); and

- Business information we receive when you (or the company you work for or represent) establish(es) or maintain(s) a business relationship with us as customer, supplier or other business partner, including for example (i) name, address, contact information, (ii) work title, business function, and related business information, and (iii) transaction information and history (including shipping and billing information, bank account/credit card information used for payment, business interactions, support experience, and product usage and preferences).

We collect the information either directly from you or from other sources, such as the company you work for or represent, other TI group companies, our external business partners, or from publicly available sources, information databases or credit agencies.
