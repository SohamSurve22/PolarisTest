##### Automated License Plate Recognition (“ALPR”) Usage and Privacy Policy

Where allowed by law, Union Pacific may operate and use ALPR systems at certain of its intermodal facilities.

These ALPR systems use cameras to capture images of vehicles and their license plates, along with the date, time, and general location, and, with the assistance of computer algorithms that read and convert this information, create searchable computerized databases. We use these ALPR systems and collect, access, and use ALPR information for security/safety, asset protection, and fraud prevention purposes, as well as to protect the rights and property of Union Pacific and others (each, an “Authorized Purpose” and together, the “Authorized Purposes”).

Only authorized employees, contractors, and authorized vendors are permitted to use and access ALPR systems and collect ALPR information (“Authorized Personnel”), and solely for Authorized Purposes. All Authorized Personnel are required to keep confidential and limit appropriate collection, access, and use of ALPR systems and information.

In order to ensure the security of ALPR information and compliance with applicable privacy laws, Union Pacific monitors its ALPR systems. In addition, Union Pacific maintains records of access to ALPR information, which includes the date and time of access, the data elements used to query the ALPR system, the identity of the person who accessed the information, and the Authorized Purpose(s) for such access. Union Pacific also may conduct periodic system audits.

Union Pacific takes reasonable measures to ensure the accuracy of ALPR information and to correct any data errors that may exist. If any errors are identified, they are promptly corrected or removed.

In no event will Union Pacific sell ALPR information to outside third parties. However, there may be limited circumstances in which we share or transfer ALPR information, including with: (i) affiliates and suppliers/service providers that assist Union Pacific, consistent with the Authorized Purposes listed above; (ii) law enforcement officials, public health officials, and other government authorities in order to comply with Union Pacific’s legal duties (e.g., in response to a warrant or other legal process), and/or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Union Pacific, its customers, and others; and (iii) other third parties, including legal advisors, to enforce our agreements and policies and protect our rights and property and the rights and property of others.
