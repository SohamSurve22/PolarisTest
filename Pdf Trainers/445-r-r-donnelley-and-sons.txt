R.R. Donnelley & Sons Company (RRD) is a global provider of marketing, packaging, print and supply chain solutions. These solutions are provided by R.R. Donnelley & Sons Company and the Valassis family of companies (Valassis Communications, Inc., Valassis Direct Mail, Inc., Valassis Digital Corp., ValassisList.com, Save.com, and NCH Marketing) that are owned by RRD.

## R.R. Donnelley & Sons Company

RRD provides Business to Business services, including:

-
Packaging

- Packaging design

- Packaging Materials and Enhancements

- Carton assembly

- Labels

- Signage and Displays

-
Print

- Customer Directed Printing

- Spend Consulting

- Business Communications

-
Supply Chain Solutions

- Kitting & Fulfillment

- Product Configuration

- Sourcing & Inventory

Our Privacy Policy related to these solutions can be found here.

## Valassis Companies

Valassis provides Marketing and Coupon Processing services, including:

-
Marketing Solutions

- Customer data management, insights, analytics, and measurement

- Custom audience creation and licensing

- Omnichannel advertising solutions, including print and digital advertising, email and SMS marketing, as well as digital coupons

- Demand-side platform (DSP) services for advertisers to buy digital advertising

- Licensing of Valassis Lists for advertising or approved analytical purposes

- Consumers marketing emails through Save.com

-
Coupon Processing Solutions

- Coupon audit, settlement, analysis, and fraud management services
