# Privacy Center

We care about your privacy and want to help you understand how we collect, use, and share your personal information.

## Your choices become our responsibilities

At Arrow, making life better with technology is good for business and for our global community. Applying the power of technology and innovation to address the world’s economic, social, and environmental challenges continues to drive us as we guide innovation forward for thousands of leading technology manufacturers and service providers.

Given our global scope and many valued relationships, Arrow works hard to earn and keep the trust of stakeholders. Transparency is essential in demonstrating our commitment to ethical business practices. Our priority is to give you agency over your information, to see it, to understand how it’s used, learn whom we might share it with and why, and make sure you are in charge of it all.
