By using the Services, you agree to the collection, use, and disclosure of your information as described in this Privacy Policy, and agree to the KDP Terms of Use, which are incorporated by reference.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to information that is collected by any third-party websites or services that you may access through the Services.

There are several ways we may obtain information about you, including through (A) information you provide to us directly; (B) information that we automatically collect; and (C) information we receive from third parties. Some of the information directly or indirectly identifies you as an individual. When information identifies or is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with you or your household, we refer to it as “Personal Information.” The Personal Information we collect about you includes the following categories:

• Audio and Visual Information:
This includes photos you share from your devices and recorded conversations with KDP representatives where permitted by law.

• Biometric information:
THair color, eye color, and other details from your face when you submit a photograph, voice, and other biometric data.

• Characteristics of protected classifications:
Your race, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, age.

• Commercial Information:
Your purchase and usage history (such as beverage variety, brew size and strength preference for your brews), goods and service preferences, and payment information.

These include your name, account name, postal and email address, phone number, physical characteristics or description, and unique identifiers that may identify your device or browser but not you by name.

• Internet/Electronic Activity:
IP address, web/app browsing and search history related to our Services, and information regarding your interaction(s) with the Services or KDP advertising.

• Geolocation Data: Data about the location of the device you use to access the Services as more fully described below.

• Profile Inferences: The inferences that we draw from your Personal Information and online activity to create a personalized profile so we can better identify goods and services that may be of interest.
