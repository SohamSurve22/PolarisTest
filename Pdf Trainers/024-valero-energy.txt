For most users accessing the Internet from a dial-up Internet service provider (ISP), the IP address will be different every time you log on.

We use your IP address to help diagnose service or technology problems with our server, and to administer our Site. Aggregate information derived from IP addresses may also be reported to advertisers. Your IP address is used to help identify you, to track activity within our Site and to gather broad demographic information which is used to further develop advertisements, products and services.

Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Valero Energy Corporation and its affiliates (collectively “Valero”) uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on Valero’s websites and digital properties.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to store data that can be recalled by a web server in the domain that placed the cookie. Cookies allow us to recognize your device, store your preferences, and improve your user experience.

We also use similar technologies like pixel tags, local storage, and analytics scripts that may collect information such as browser type, device ID, IP address, and usage patterns.

Types of Cookies We Use

- Strictly Necessary Cookies

- Required for core website functionality.

- Examples: Session cookies, authentication.

- Functional Cookies

- Enhance user experience by storing preferences.

- Examples: Language or region preferences.

- Performance Cookies

- Provide insights into site usage to help optimize performance.

- Examples: Google Analytics, Adobe Analytics.

- Targeting/Marketing Cookies

- Enable personalization and deliver relevant ads.

- Examples: Meta Pixel, Google Ads.

Legal Bases and Consent

We are committed to meeting local legal obligations in respect of our use of cookies. In all locations, we will obtain your prior consent for non-essential cookies before they are deployed.

When you first visit our Site, a cookie banner or settings tool will guide you in choosing which cookies to allow or reject.

How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences by:

- Using our cookie settings tool.

- Adjusting browser settings to block or delete cookies.

- Using opt-out tools provided by third-party providers. Please note that disabling cookies may impact functionality.

Data Sharing and Retention

We may share cookie-derived data with trusted third-party service providers for analytics, advertising, or platform integration. We retain cookie data only as long as necessary to fulfil its intended purposes and comply with legal obligations.
