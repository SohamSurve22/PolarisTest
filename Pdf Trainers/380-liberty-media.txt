The types of Personal Information that we collect or receive about you depend on how you interact with us. We have set out below the types of Personal Information we may collect by the different sources of information:

We may collect Personal Information directly from you, such as your full name, email address, telephone number, and any other Personal Information you may provide to us. We may also collect business contact data, professional experience, and background data.

Personal Information We Collect Automatically.

When you visit the Site, we may automatically collect certain types of information from you, including Personal Information. This information may include computer and connection information, such as statistics on your page views, traffic to and from our Site, referral URL, your IP address, and device identifiers. This information also may include your browsing history, transaction history, and your web log information. We may also collect information using tracking technologies such as cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, and similar technologies. You can learn more about these tracking tools in the “Tracking Technologies” section below.
