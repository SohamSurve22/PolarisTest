### Cookies & Tracking Pixels

A cookie is a very small text file that a Web page server sends to your hard drive, in response to your browser's request for a Web page. The purpose of the cookie is to help a website server recognize a user. Sites with secured information use cookies to help recognize User IDs, or on our site, to help users navigate between secured and unsecured information. Cookies also help website managers track which information on their site is most useful and most often accessed, and which pages are seldom visited. The cookie for our site, recognizable only to our server, simply helps to identify you and tell us which information you wish to read, and which you don't. To the extent permitted by your browser, you may opt out of cookie tracking.

Union Pacific also collects information related to the effectiveness of our digital advertisements. Specifically, Union Pacific works with third-party companies that use tracking technologies (tracking pixels) on our website in order to serve interest-based ads on our behalf across the internet. These companies may collect information about your activity on our sites and your interaction with our advertising and use this information to determine which ads you see on third-party websites and applications. For more information about this practice and to understand your options including how to opt out of receiving these tailored advertisements, please visit http://www.aboutads.info.

##### Automated License Plate Recognition (“ALPR”) Usage and Privacy Policy

Where allowed by law, Union Pacific may operate and use ALPR systems at certain of its intermodal facilities.

These ALPR systems use cameras to capture images of vehicles and their license plates, along with the date, time, and general location, and, with the assistance of computer algorithms that read and convert this information, create searchable computerized databases. We use these ALPR systems and collect, access, and use ALPR information for security/safety, asset protection, and fraud prevention purposes, as well as to protect the rights and property of Union Pacific and others (each, an “Authorized Purpose” and together, the “Authorized Purposes”).

Only authorized employees, contractors, and authorized vendors are permitted to use and access ALPR systems and collect ALPR information (“Authorized Personnel”), and solely for Authorized Purposes. All Authorized Personnel are required to keep confidential and limit appropriate collection, access, and use of ALPR systems and information.

In order to ensure the security of ALPR information and compliance with applicable privacy laws, Union Pacific monitors its ALPR systems. In addition, Union Pacific maintains records of access to ALPR information, which includes the date and time of access, the data elements used to query the ALPR system, the identity of the person who accessed the information, and the Authorized Purpose(s) for such access. Union Pacific also may conduct periodic system audits.

Union Pacific takes reasonable measures to ensure the accuracy of ALPR information and to correct any data errors that may exist. If any errors are identified, they are promptly corrected or removed.

In no event will Union Pacific sell ALPR information to outside third parties. However, there may be limited circumstances in which we share or transfer ALPR information, including with: (i) affiliates and suppliers/service providers that assist Union Pacific, consistent with the Authorized Purposes listed above; (ii) law enforcement officials, public health officials, and other government authorities in order to comply with Union Pacific’s legal duties (e.g., in response to a warrant or other legal process), and/or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Union Pacific, its customers, and others; and (iii) other third parties, including legal advisors, to enforce our agreements and policies and protect our rights and property and the rights and property of others. When we do disclose ALPR information to third parties, we require outside parties that receive ALPR information from Union Pacific to handle such information in accordance with our contract terms (including applicable confidentiality obligations), relevant laws, and industry best practices, and prohibit them from selling this information or using it for any commercial purpose.

Union Pacific will retain ALPR information for as long as necessary for the Authorized Purposes, and destroy ALPR information when we determine its retention is no longer necessary, unless we are required to retain the ALPR information to comply with legal processes or to satisfy other legal requirements.

Union Pacific’s Chief Privacy Officer is the official custodian responsible for overseeing Union Pacific’s ALPR systems, ensuring compliance with this policy and applicable law, and coordinating with our technology and legal teams for periodic reviews.
