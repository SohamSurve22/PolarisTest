## Northwestern Mutual Online Services Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 29, 2026

Printable PDF of the Online Privacy Policy

The Northwestern Mutual Family of Companies works together to serve customers and enable us to provide holistic financial products and services. This Privacy Policy describes how The Northwestern Mutual Family of Companies may collect your Personal Information when you interact with our Online Services. It also provides information about how we use and disclose Personal Information, the choices we allow, how we protect Personal Information, and how you may contact us about our privacy practices.

Within this Policy, "Northwestern Mutual Family of Companies,""We," or "Our" means the Northwestern Mutual Life Insurance Company, Northwestern Long Term Care Insurance Company, Northwestern Mutual Investment Services, LLC, Northwestern Mutual Wealth Management Company, and the network offices of our sales force. Our sales force includes the agents who sell Our products and services along with their staff.

Our "Online Services" include the Websites ("Sites") and mobile applications ("Mobile Apps") of the Northwestern Mutual Family of Companies. Online Services also include voice recordings, emails, online chat, text messages, and other electronic communications between you and the Northwestern Mutual Family of Companies.

Information collected through Our Online Services may be retained by Northwestern Mutual or Our Service Providers. This information may be monitored for regulatory and compliance purposes and may also be used for Our business purposes as described below.

Also, when you interact with, submit, or send information to us through Our Online Services, this information may be shared among the Northwestern Mutual Family of Companies. Your choices about how you may be able to limit this sharing are described below.

You may view additional information about Our privacy practices by reviewing Our other Privacy Notices including Our California Notice at Collection. Also, our SMS text messaging Terms and Conditions can be found here.

When you interact with Our Online Services, you are agreeing to the terms of this Policy and Our other Privacy Notices.
