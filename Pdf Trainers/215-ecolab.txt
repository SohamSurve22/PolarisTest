## What Personal Information Do We Collect About You?

Personal information (also commonly known as personally identifiable information (PII) or personal data) is information that can be used to identify you, or any other individual to whom the information may relate.

The personal information that we collect directly from those accessing the Sites and Services include the following categories:

- Name and contact information (e.g. address; phone number; email, fax);

- Account information (e.g. login credentials – username, password);

- Order Information (e.g. current order/purchase information, purchase history, shipping details);

- Certain cookies or tracking technologies that may be considered personal information or personal data under applicable law. More information is available in the Cookie Policy;

- Audio and visual information (e.g. CCTV recordings when you visit one of Ecolab’s offices, or call recordings when you contact our customer support team);

- Inferences we generate about you or derive from any of the information we collect that relate to your preferences.

## Opt-Out Preference Signals

The Sites and Services associated with www.ecolab.com recognize the Global Privacy Control (“GPC”) signal. Please note that, while this Privacy Policy applies across all websites, the applicability of cookies and the affiliated cookie Policy may vary if you are on certain websites outside of www.ecolab.com.

If you are using a browser setting or plug-in that sends an opt-out preference signal to each website you visit, we will treat that as a valid request to opt out. To download and use a browser supporting the GPC browser signal, click here: https://globalprivacycontrol.org. If you choose to use the GPC signal, you will need to turn it on for each supported browser or browser extension you use.

Some internet browsers incorporate a “Do Not Track” feature that signals to websites you visit that you do not want to have your online activity tracked. Given that there is not a uniform way that browsers communicate the “Do Not Track” signal, the Site does not currently interpret, respond to or alter its practices when it receives “Do Not Track” signals.
